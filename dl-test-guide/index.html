Telangana Permanent Driving Licence (DL) Track Test & Slot Booking Guide

Telangana RTA Permanent DL guide: Two-Wheeler 8-Track & Car H-Track rules, automated sensor tracks, Parivahan slot booking & DigiLocker LLR.

1. Telangana RTA Online Application, Slot Booking & Digital LLR Download

  1. Step 1: Apply Online on Telangana Transport Portal

    Visit transport.telangana.gov.in -> Online Services -> Driving Licence -> Fresh Learner's Licence (LLR). Verify with Aadhaar OTP, choose vehicle classes (MCWG / LMV), and submit Form 2 & self-declaration Form 1.

  2. Step 2: Choose Contactless Aadhaar Online Test or RTO Slot

    Pay ₹200 (1 class) or ₹350 (2 classes) online. With Aadhaar e-KYC on Parivahan Sarathi (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in), eligible applicants can take the LLR test online from a webcam-equipped PC using AI facial recognition & Safe Exam Browser—or book a physical RTO STALL booth slot (TS/TG-07 to TS/TG-12) and print Form 2 + Slot Slip.

  3. Step 3: Download Digital LLR & Add to DigiLocker / mParivahan

    Within ~15 minutes of scoring 12/20, you receive an SMS link to download your signed PDF LLR. Fetch it into DigiLocker or NextGen mParivahan—digital documents are legally valid across India.

2. Permanent Driving Licence (DL) Track Test Guide (After 30 Days)

You can book your Permanent DL track test slot on transport.telangana.gov.in after completing 30 days from your LLR issue date (valid up to 180 days). Major Hyderabad/Telangana RTA tracks (Kondapur TS-07/TG-07, Uppal TS-08/TG-08, Medchal, Nagole, Ibrahimpatnam) use automated RFID sensors & HD cameras to score track accuracy objectively. Before booking your Permanent DL slot, complete the mandatory online Road Safety Training module on tgtransport.online/llr/ if prompted.

MCWG / Two-Wheeler

Figure '8' Track Test (Motorcycle / Scooter)

  • Wear an ISI-certified strapped helmet before starting.
  • Ride smoothly along the figure-8 loop using steady clutch & throttle control.
  • Do NOT put either foot down on the ground inside the track.
  • Do NOT touch or cross the painted curb/sensor lines.
  • Use turn indicators when entering and exiting the track.
LMV / Four-Wheeler

The 'H' Track & Reversing Test (Car / LMV)

  • Adjust seat & side mirrors and fasten your seatbelt before moving.
  • Drive forward into the 'H' bay and reverse cleanly through the narrow lane without hitting poles or boundary lines.
  • Use side rear-view mirrors and check blind spots while reversing.
  • Avoid stalling the engine or rolling backward on gradients/ramps.
  • Signal properly with indicators before every turn or reverse maneuver.

3. Documents & Test Vehicle Checklist for DL Track Day

  • Original Valid Learner's Licence (LLR) — at least 30 days old
  • Printed DL Track Test Slot Booking Receipt
  • Original Aadhaar Card + 1 photocopy & passport photos
  • Test Vehicle with valid RC, Insurance, PUC & visible 'L' Sticker

Permanent DL Track Test FAQ

When can I apply for a Permanent Driving Licence (DL) after passing the LLR test?

You must hold your valid LLR for at least 30 days and apply before it expires at 180 days (6 months).

What causes instant failure on Telangana automated Figure-8 and H tracks?

Putting a foot down on the bike Figure-8 track, hitting sensor poles/curbs, stalling on gradients, or forgetting helmet/seatbelt and turn indicators.

Is the online Road Safety Training module mandatory before DL slot booking?

Telangana Transport provides an online Road Safety Training module at tgtransport.online/llr/—complete it before booking your DL track slot if prompted on your application.
