About, Official Disclaimer & Contact

About Telangana RTA LLR Practice by Sarva Capital: free educational mission, official Telangana Transport curriculum attribution, non-affiliation disclaimer, and contact details.

1. Mission & Educational Purpose

Telangana RTA Learner's Licence Practice (llr.sarvacapital.in) is a free civic-education web application built by Sarva Capital to help learner drivers across Hyderabad and all 33+ districts of Telangana prepare thoroughly for the computerized STALL (Screen Test Aid for Learner Licence) exam and build lifelong road safety habits.

2. Non-Government Affiliation Disclaimer

Independent Practice Tool: This website is NOT an official website of, nor affiliated with, the Government of Telangana Transport Department (RTA) or Parivahan Sarathi. All official Learner's Licence applications, fee payments, slot bookings, and digital LLR downloads must be completed exclusively on the official government portal: transport.telangana.gov.in.

3. Official Curriculum & Source Attribution

The 412 questions (94 Road Signs, 159 Rules of the Road Regulations, and 159 General Driving Principles), fixed option sequence, 8-8-4 category quota, and 30-second countdown logic are derived directly from the publicly published Telangana Transport Department STALL curriculum under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.

4. Terms of Use

While we carefully verify all translations, answer keys, RTO office addresses, and Motor Vehicles Act fine schedules, statutory rules and RTO fees may change over time. Always verify current fee schedules and slot guidelines directly on official government notices.

5. Contact & Feedback

Found a typo in Telugu/Hindi text, an outdated RTO office detail, or want to share feedback? Reach our team at contact@sarvacapital.in.
