Step-by-Step Online LLR Application & Test Flow

  1. Step 1: Apply Online on Telangana Transport Portal

    Visit transport.telangana.gov.in -> Online Services -> Driving Licence -> Fresh Learner's Licence (LLR). Verify with Aadhaar OTP, choose vehicle classes (MCWG / LMV), and submit Form 2 & self-declaration Form 1.

  2. Step 2: Choose Contactless Aadhaar Online Test or RTO Slot

    Pay ₹200 (1 class) or ₹350 (2 classes) online. With Aadhaar e-KYC on Parivahan Sarathi (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in), eligible applicants can take the LLR test online from a webcam-equipped PC using AI facial recognition & Safe Exam Browser—or book a physical RTO STALL booth slot (TS/TG-07 to TS/TG-12) and print Form 2 + Slot Slip.

  3. Step 3: Download Digital LLR & Add to DigiLocker / mParivahan

    Within ~15 minutes of scoring 12/20, you receive an SMS link to download your signed PDF LLR. Fetch it into DigiLocker or NextGen mParivahan—digital documents are legally valid across India.

Online LLR Test FAQ

Can I take the Telangana LLR test online from home without visiting the RTO?

Yes. Applicants using Aadhaar e-KYC authentication on the Parivahan Sarathi portal (sarathi.parivahan.gov.in) can take the contactless online LLR test from a webcam-equipped laptop or desktop using AI facial recognition and Safe Exam Browser.

When is an in-person visit to the Hyderabad or Telangana RTO still required?

You must visit your designated RTO STALL booth if you apply without Aadhaar e-KYC, have name/date-of-birth mismatches on Aadhaar, do not have a webcam-equipped computer, or encounter facial-liveness verification failures.

Does this simulator prepare me for both Sarathi online LLR and physical RTO STALL tests?

Yes. Both modes test the same core Motor Vehicles Act / Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR) road signs, traffic rules, and safe driving principles with 30 seconds per question and a 60% pass requirement. Our 412 questions are sourced directly from the official Telangana Transport Department bank.

How fast do I get my Learner's Licence after passing online?

Upon passing, your digitally signed QR-coded Learner's Licence PDF is generated immediately (typically via SMS link within 15 minutes) and can be fetched in DigiLocker or NextGen mParivahan.
