mandatory Sign #89

mandatory Sign #90

DRIVER HAND SIGNAL — GIVE WAY TO BE OVERTAKEN

Official RTA Key: Option 4 ('HE INTENDS TO GIVE WAY, TO BE OVERTAKEN'). Waving arm forward signals other vehicles to overtake.

Road Signs Exam FAQ

How many road sign questions appear in the Telangana LLR STALL test?

Exactly 8 out of the 20 test questions (40% of the exam) are Road Signs and Traffic Signal questions drawn from this official 94-sign curriculum.

How can I tell Mandatory, Cautionary, and Informatory signs apart?

Mandatory signs are mostly circles (red border for prohibitions, blue circle for compulsory actions) plus STOP and GIVE WAY. Cautionary warning signs are red triangles. Informatory guide signs are blue rectangles.

Are these Telangana road signs valid for Parivahan Sarathi LLR exams across India?

Yes. All traffic signs follow the national IRC:67 and Motor Vehicles Act standard used across Parivahan Sarathi learner licence tests throughout India.
