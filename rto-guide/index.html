1. Required Documents Checklist for Telangana LLR Test

  • Application Form Printout (Form 2) Mandatory

    Printed copy of the online application submitted on transport.telangana.gov.in or Parivahan Sarathi.

  • Slot Booking Appointment Slip Mandatory

    Confirms your designated RTO office (e.g. Khairatabad, Kondapur, Uppal) and time slot.

  • Original Aadhaar Card + 1 Photocopy Mandatory

    Mandatory primary photo ID and address proof required at verification counter.

  • Age & Address Proof (SSC Memo / Passport / Voter ID) Mandatory

    Date of birth verification (minimum age 16 for gearless 50cc, 18 for LMV car/motorcycle with gear).

  • Online Payment Fee Receipt Mandatory

    Receipt showing test and service fee paid (₹150 test fee + ₹50 service fee per class).

  • Medical Certificate (Form 1 / Form 1A) Optional

    Self-declaration Form 1 is standard. Form 1A signed by a registered doctor is required if applicant is over 40.

  • Passport Size Photographs (2 copies) Optional

    Keep 2 recent color photographs with white background as backup (biometrics are captured digitally).

2. Test-Day 5-Step Walkthrough at Hyderabad RTO

  1. Step 1: Arrival & Queue Token

    Reach your designated RTO office 15-20 minutes before your slot time. Present your slot booking slip at the entrance reception counter to receive your computerized queue token.

  2. Step 2: Document Verification (MVI Counter)

    Wait for your token number on the digital display. The Motor Vehicle Inspector (MVI) verifies your original Aadhaar card, age proof, and Form 2 printout, then digitally approves your application.

  3. Step 3: Biometric & Digital Photo Capture

    Proceed to the biometric room. Your live digital photograph, digital stylus signature, and thumbprint will be captured. Note: This live photo will appear on your final driving licence!

  4. Step 4: STALL Computer Test Booth

    Enter the air-conditioned STALL test lab. Sit at your assigned terminal. Enter your Application / Token number. Select your preferred test language (English, Telugu, or Hindi). Put on headphones if you wish to hear audio prompts. Answer 20 questions (30 seconds per question).

  5. Step 5: Instant Result & LLR PDF Download

    The moment you reach 12 correct answers, the computer screen displays 'CONGRATULATIONS - TEST PASSED'. An SMS with the official digital LLR download link is sent to your registered mobile within 15 minutes. No physical paper collection needed!

4. Official LLR Fee Structure, Retest & Validity Rules

Vehicle Class Fee Breakdown Total Fee
One Vehicle Class (e.g., Two-Wheeler MCWG) ₹150 LLR Test Fee + ₹50 Service Fee ₹200
Two Vehicle Classes (Two-Wheeler + Car LMV) ₹300 Test Fee (₹150 x 2) + ₹50 Service Fee ₹350

Retest Policy: If you fail the test, you can book a re-test slot after 7 days on transport.telangana.gov.in by paying a re-test fee of ₹150.

LLR Validity & Permanent DL Rule: Your Learner's Licence (LLR) is valid for 6 months across India. You are eligible to apply for your permanent Driving Licence (DL) driving track test after completing 30 days from LLR issue date.

5. Top Tips to Pass on Your First Attempt

Don't Panic at the 30-Second Timer

30 seconds is plenty of time to read 1 short question and 4 options. Spend 10 seconds carefully reading before clicking.

Watch out for Negative Words ('NOT', 'NEVER')

Over 40% of candidate mistakes occur on questions asking 'Where must you NOT park?' or 'When should you NEVER overtake?'. Read every word cautiously.

Master the Road Sign Shapes

Circles = Mandatory Orders (Law - Must Obey). Triangles = Cautionary Warnings (Hazards ahead). Rectangles/Blue = Informatory Guidance (Directions, amenities).

Aim for 12 Out of 12 Early Finish

If you get the first 12 questions right, the computer instantly passes you and closes the test. You don't even have to face the remaining 8 questions!

Hyderabad & Telangana RTO FAQ

What is the LLR fee in Telangana for bike and car together?

For one vehicle class (e.g., MCWG motorcycle), the fee is ₹200 (₹150 test fee + ₹50 service fee). For two classes (e.g., Two-Wheeler + Car LMV), the fee is ₹350 (₹300 + ₹50 service fee).

How many days after failing the Telangana LLR test can I book a retest?

You can book a retest slot after 7 days on transport.telangana.gov.in by paying a ₹150 retest fee.

How long is a Telangana Learner's Licence valid?

A Learner's Licence is valid for 6 months (180 days) across India. You become eligible for the Permanent Driving Licence (DL) track test after completing 30 days from LLR issue.
