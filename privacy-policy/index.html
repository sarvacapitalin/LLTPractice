Privacy Policy & Cookie Notice

Privacy Policy for llr.sarvacapital.in explaining local browser storage, zero personal data collection, and Google AdSense cookie disclosures.

Last updated: 2026-11-25 • Domain: llr.sarvacapital.in

1. Overview & Zero-Account Local Practice

Telangana RTA Learner's Licence Practice (operated by Sarva Capital at llr.sarvacapital.in) is a free public educational simulator. We do not require registration, logins, phone numbers, Aadhaar details, or personal profiles to use any practice mode, mock test, or study guide.

2. Browser Local Storage & Cross-Device QR Sync

Your quiz streaks, readiness progress, language preference (English, Telugu, or Hindi), sound settings, and RTO document checklist ticks are stored exclusively inside your own browser's localStorage. None of your study scores are uploaded to any backend database. When you use the optional QR Cross-Device Sync feature, your compact study progress is encoded directly inside the sync URL / JSON export file processed locally in your browser.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

To keep this learning platform 100% free for students and learner drivers across Telangana, we plan to display non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdSense on study and post-exam summary screens (never inside the active 30-second timed mock test).

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to llr.sarvacapital.in and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or opt out of third-party vendor cookie use via aboutads.info.

4. External Official Links

We link to official government portals such as transport.telangana.gov.in for application filing, slot booking, and DigiLocker/mParivahan verification. External sites operate under their own privacy policies.

5. Contact Us

For privacy questions, ad inquiries, or content corrections, please write to contact@sarvacapital.in.
