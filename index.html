About Telangana RTA Learner's Licence (LLR) STALL Examination Simulator

This simulator is an authentic, free educational tool designed to help driving licence candidates in Hyderabad and throughout Telangana pass their computerized STALL (Screen Test Aid for Learner Licence) exam on their first attempt. The question bank comprises all 412 official questions released by the Telangana Transport Department under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, categorized into 94 Road Signs, 159 Rules of the Road, and 159 General Driving Principles.

Exam Format: 20 Multiple Choice Questions (8 Signs, 8 Rules, 4 Principles)
Passing Criteria: 12 correct out of 20 (60%). Zero negative marking.
Timer: 30 seconds per question (10 minutes total).
Languages: English, Telugu (తెలుగు), and Hindi (हिन्दी) with audio read-aloud.

Frequently Asked Questions (Telangana LLR & RTO Hyderabad)

What happens when I get 12 correct answers in the official STALL exam?

In the official Telangana RTA STALL exam, the computerized kiosk immediately stops as soon as you record your 12th correct answer and declares you as "PASSED". You do not have to answer the remaining questions. If 9 questions are answered incorrectly, the test stops and marks "FAILED".

Are the 4 answer options shuffled in the real RTO test?

No. The options (1, 2, 3, 4) for any given question remain in the exact same fixed sequence as in the government master question bank. The physical kiosk keypad buttons (1, 2, 3, 4) map directly to these options. Only the selection of the 20 questions is randomized per candidate.

Which Hyderabad RTO offices conduct the STALL computerized test?

The STALL test is conducted at all Regional Transport Offices across Greater Hyderabad including RTO Khairatabad (TS-09), RTO Secunderabad (TS-10), RTO Malakpet (TS-12), RTO Mehdipatnam / Tolichowki (TS-11), RTO Chandrayangutta (TS-13), RTO Kondapur / Cyberabad (TS-07), and RTO Uppal (TS-08).
