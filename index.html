About Telangana RTA Learner's Licence (LLR) STALL Examination Simulator

This simulator is an authentic, free educational tool designed to help driving licence candidates in Hyderabad and throughout Telangana pass their computerized STALL (Screen Test Aid for Learner Licence) exam on their first attempt. The question bank comprises all 412 official questions released by the Telangana Transport Department under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, categorized into 94 Road Signs, 159 Rules of the Road, and 159 General Driving Principles.

Exam Format: 20 Multiple Choice Questions (8 Signs, 8 Rules, 4 Principles)
Passing Criteria: 12 correct out of 20 (60%). Zero negative marking.
Timer: 30 seconds per question (10 minutes total).
Language: English (official RTA wording with audio read-aloud)

Frequently Asked Questions (Telangana LLR & RTO Hyderabad)

How many questions are asked in the Telangana RTA Learner's Licence (LLR) STALL test?

The Telangana RTA STALL (Screen Test Aid for Learner Licence) test consists of exactly 20 multiple-choice questions: 8 Road Signs (40%), 8 Rules of the Road (40%), and 4 General Driving Principles (20%).

What is the pass mark for the Telangana LLR computerized exam?

The pass mark is 12 out of 20 (60%). There is no negative marking for incorrect answers.

What is the time limit for each question in the Telangana STALL exam?

Candidates are allotted 30 seconds per question, with a maximum total test duration of 10 minutes.

In which languages can I take the Telangana LLR exam?

At official Telangana RTA offices, the exam is offered in English, Telugu, and Hindi. This practice simulator currently runs in English with official RTA question wording and audio read-aloud (Telugu and Hindi modes are temporarily paused while translations are audited).

Does the exam stop early when a candidate scores 12 correct answers?

Yes. In the official STALL exam system at Hyderabad and Telangana RTOs, as soon as a candidate reaches 12 correct answers, the test immediately finishes and displays 'PASSED'. If a candidate gets 9 wrong answers, the test concludes with 'FAILED'.

Are the 4 answer options shuffled in the mock test?

Yes. Answer choices (1 to 4) are shuffled per question by default to match the latest STALL exam format and ensure you learn the actual traffic rule rather than option positions.

Which Hyderabad RTO offices conduct the STALL computerized test?

The STALL test is conducted at all Regional Transport Offices across Greater Hyderabad including RTO Khairatabad (TS-09 / TG-09), RTO Secunderabad (TS-10 / TG-10), RTO Malakpet (TS-12 / TG-12), RTO Mehdipatnam / Tolichowki (TS-11 / TG-11), RTO Chandrayangutta (TS-13 / TG-13), RTO Kondapur / Cyberabad (TS-07 / TG-07), and RTO Uppal (TS-08 / TG-08).
